The business diagnostic

Six scores. One honest picture.

The Funding Readiness Assessment tells you whether you can borrow. The Business Diagnostic goes deeper: a consultant works through the whole business with you and scores six areas, each producing its own recommendations.

It is the engine behind everything else we do — the clinics you are sent to, the advisory work quoted, and the courses recommended all come out of this.
