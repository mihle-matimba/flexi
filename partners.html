The agent model

A partnership, not employment.

Agents earn commission on every funded deal and a fee on every paid clinic they host. There is no salary, no fixed hours and no territory quota — what you earn tracks what you build.

  • Commission per funded deal, paid on disbursement
  • A fee per paid seat at clinics you host
  • Referral income across advisory, accounting and training
  • Training and accreditation through Aptivo Institute
  • A tablet toolkit that works offline in the field
What the portal gives you

Everything runs from a tablet.

  • Digital lead and application capture, offline-tolerant
  • Run readiness assessments on-site with clients
  • Schedule clinics and take paid bookings
  • Live commission statements per funded deal
  • Training content served from Aptivo Institute
