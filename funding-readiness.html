Questions

Common questions

Does this affect my credit record?

No. The assessment is not a credit application and no credit bureau enquiry is made. A J-Cred bureau check only happens once you submit an actual funding application and consent to it.

What does it cost?

The assessment is free. Services it recommends — advisory work, business clinics, accredited courses — are priced individually and always quoted before you commit.

Can I re-take it?

Yes, as often as you like. Most clients re-take it after completing advisory or a clinic to see their score move. Your dashboard keeps the history.

Can an agent do this with me?

Yes. Community Business Development Agents run the same assessment on a tablet, on-site with you. Find one through Partners & agents.
