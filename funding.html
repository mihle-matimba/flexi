Responsible lending

FlexiGrowth Capital is a registered credit provider (NCRCP23507). Every application is subject to affordability assessment, identity and AML verification, and a credit bureau check in terms of the National Credit Act. Approval, amount and pricing depend on the outcome of that assessment. Terms and conditions apply.

How a decision gets made

From application to payout.

1

Apply

A short mobile application, self-serve or captured by an agent on a tablet.

2

Verify

Automated AML and identity verification, plus a J-Cred credit bureau check.

3

Assess

FlexiScore™ blends bureau data with cashflow, savings behaviour, stokvel participation and contract verification.

4

Decide

Credit committee decision, then contracting and payout through our loan management system.

5

Support

Advisory and enterprise development support for the life of the facility.
