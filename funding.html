What the questionnaireasks about.

Nothing needs to be uploaded yet. Every question can be answered from memory, and each section counts towards your score by the weight shown.

SECTIONS ONE & TWO

About the business

15% of your score
  • Registration, entity type and sector
  • Years trading, employees and turnover
  • Who owns and runs the business, and what happens if they are away
SECTION THREE

What you need

15% of your score
  • The funding product and amount
  • The term you would like
  • What the money is for, and how soon
SECTIONS FOUR TO SIX

Market, money & compliance

50% of your score
  • Your market, and how concentrated your revenue is
  • Financial records, bank statements and a forecast
  • CIPC, SARS and any licences you need
SECTIONS SEVEN & EIGHT

Operations & repayment

20% of your score
  • How the business runs day to day
  • Existing debt, and where repayment will come from
  • Your details, documents and consent

Questions

Common questions

Straight answers before you begin.

Does this affect my credit record?

No. Answering the questionnaire does not touch your credit record. A J-Cred bureau check only runs once our team takes your application forward, and only with the consent you give at the end.

What does it cost?

The questionnaire is free. Any services it recommends — advisory work, business clinics or accredited courses — are priced individually and always quoted before you commit.

Can I retake it?

Yes, as often as you like. Most clients retake it after advisory work or a clinic, to see their score move. Our team sees every result, and your advisor takes you through the latest one.

Can an agent complete it with me?

Yes. Community Business Development Agents run the same questionnaire with you on a tablet, on-site. Find one through Partners & agents.
