The FlexiGrowth journey

Banks decide. We develop.

A traditional lender runs Apply → Credit check → Approved or declined. That leaves most South African businesses on the wrong side of a yes or no. Our sequence is built to move you across it.

01 · Assess

Funding Readiness

A single online assessment scores your business out of 100 and shows exactly what is missing.

02 · Improve

Fix the basics

Advisory, compliance work, clinics and coaching close the gaps the assessment found.

03 · Prepare

Get application ready

Statements, tax clearance and cashflow forecasts pulled together into a fundable pack.

04 · Fund

Apply and draw down

FlexiScore™ assessment, credit decision and payout through our loan management system.

05 · Support

Capital with capability

Every funded client keeps an advisory and ESD relationship for the life of the facility.

06 · Grow

Track business health

Your dashboard tracks readiness, compliance and growth scores as the business improves.

07 · Reinvest

Qualify for more

A stronger score unlocks a higher funding limit and the next stage of growth.

The client journey

Apply from any phone.

No branch visit, no paperwork queue. Apply yourself on the site, or have a Community Business Development Agent capture the application with you on a tablet.

  • 1

    Apply

    A short mobile application — self-serve online or captured by an agent in the field.

  • 2

    Verify

    Automated AML and identity verification plus a J-Cred credit bureau check run in the background.

  • 3

    Assess

    FlexiScore™ blends bureau data with cashflow, savings behaviour, stokvel participation and contract verification.

  • 4

    Decide & disburse

    Approved deals flow into our loan management system for contracting and payout.

  • 5

    Support

    Every funded client is linked to advisory and enterprise development support.
